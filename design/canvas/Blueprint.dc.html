FIG. 1 — WHAT A REVIEW COVERS

A pull request stops at your last commit. Your work doesn't.

GitWarren finds the worktree where your branch is checked out — wherever it lives on disk — and folds everything in it into the diff. Local only: no server, no account, nothing cached.
